Boden Powers

Boden Powers was born during a storm so violent that the power grid in his hometown failed for three days straight. Nurses would later swear that the lights flickered back on the exact moment he cried for the first time, though no one could ever prove it. He grew up in a quiet neighborhood where nothing unusual was supposed to happen. His father was an electrician, his mother a school librarian, and for most of his childhood, Boden seemed ordinary—quiet, observant, a little too curious for his own good. But strange things followed him. Streetlights dimmed when he walked under them. Old radios crackled to life when he entered a room. Once, during a school blackout, every computer in the building rebooted simultaneously while he sat alone in the hallway. At first, people blamed faulty wiring, coincidence, or bad luck. Boden blamed himself. When he was twelve, he discovered the truth by accident. Frustrated after failing to fix a broken game console, he slammed his hand onto it—and felt something surge out of him, like a current snapping loose. The console roared back to life instantly, brighter and faster than it had ever been. That night, every electronic device in the house flickered on and off in chaotic rhythm, as if responding to his pulse. Terrified, he tried to suppress it. He avoided touching anything electrical, wore rubber gloves, and kept his distance from anything with a battery or a wire. It worked… for a while. But power doesn’t like being contained.

As he grew older, the energy inside him became harder to ignore. Emotions made it worse—anger caused lights to shatter, fear triggered blackouts, and excitement made sparks dance across his fingertips. By fifteen, he could no longer pretend it was random. He wasn’t just affecting electricity. He was generating it. Everything changed the day a citywide outage hit during a heatwave. Hospitals went dark, traffic lights failed, and panic spread fast. Boden felt it immediately—not just the absence of power, but a kind of silence, like something missing from the air. Without fully understanding why, he stepped outside, closed his eyes, and let go of the control he’d been forcing on himself for years. The grid came back in seconds. No one saw him do it. No one connected the sudden restoration to the quiet boy standing barefoot on the sidewalk. But Boden knew. Now he walks a careful line between hiding and helping. He keeps his abilities secret, using them only when he thinks no one will notice—stabilizing failing systems, preventing outages, quietly fixing what breaks. But each time he does, the energy inside him grows stronger, less predictable. Sometimes, when he looks at power lines humming overhead, he feels like they’re calling to him. And deep down, Boden Powers wonders if he’s just someone who controls electricity… —or if he’s something the world wasn’t meant to handle at all.
